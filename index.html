About WIL & COMPANY

WIL & COMPANY Insolvency Practitioners is a professional practice based in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe.

We aim to provide practical, reliable and professional assistance to individuals, businesses and organisations facing financial, corporate and legal challenges.

Our approach is built on professionalism, integrity, confidentiality and a commitment to finding appropriate solutions for our clients.
